MetaCTO · Brand Relaunch 05 / 10
This is the core SKU slide. Keep it simple. ECE is the foundation. Agentic Workflows are a great first implementation. Agents are for scoped roles with boundaries. AI Operations is what keeps the thing working once it is live. The ECE page describes ECE as connecting fragmented systems, structuring context, and deploying production AI systems. Agentic Workflows are positioned around shipping one workflow end to end in 4 to 6 weeks, including examples like sales briefs, proposal drafts, renewal risk, and support triage. Autonomous Agents are scoped to operational roles, built on existing tools and business context, with human oversight. Continuous AI Operations covers monitoring, evals, tuning, model upgrades, and incident response.
